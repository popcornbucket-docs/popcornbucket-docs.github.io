Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18.11.2026 · Version 3

In short: Speed Ninja needs no account. Your progress stays on your device. We ourselves receive only little data: from Google Play, details about purchases (e.g. order number, price, country) and aggregated statistics – and your details if you write to us. Data goes to Google: for the ad videos, for purchases through Google Play and – if you have switched it on – for the Android backup.

2. Overview

  • No user account, no registration, no server of our own.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own. From Google Play we only receive aggregated statistics and crash reports that are not linked to you (see 8.).
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • Permissions: internet and network state (for ad videos and Google Play), advertising ID and Android's advertising interfaces (for Google AdMob) and Google Play billing (for the purchase). None of them gives access to your personal content; you don't have to grant any of them individually.
  • Data goes to third parties only to Google (ads via AdMob, purchases and statistics via Google Play, Android backup); anyone who visits our website also to GitHub (see 14.).

3. Progress on your device

The app stores locally in your device's protected app storage: your progress per ninja (level, experience, coins, places visited and completed, jobs, scrolls, weapons, talismans, healing potions, techniques discovered), your settings (language, sound, ninja played last), which version of the terms of use and privacy policy you accepted and when, your age group (see 5.), whether you bought the videos away and which battle song plays next. Google's consent platform also stores your ad consent decision there (see 6.).

This data does not leave your device towards the provider. Its only purpose is to provide the game to you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract for the use of the app); storing it on your device is strictly necessary for the service you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Storage period: until you delete the data in the app (Legal and data → Delete game data; this also deletes the stored ad consent), clear the app data in Android's settings or uninstall the app (see Delete your data).

4. Android backup

If Android backup ("Backup by Google One") is switched on on your device, Android can back up your progress encrypted in your Google account and restore it on a new device. You control this backup yourself in Android's settings; it is provided by Google, and Google's privacy policy applies (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The legal basis for allowing the backup is our legitimate interest in sparing you the loss of your progress when you change devices (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Information at the first start (age question)

At the first start the app asks for your year of birth. From it only the age group is derived (under 13, 13–15, 16 and older) and stored locally – the year of birth itself is neither stored nor transmitted. The age group determines which ads may be requested and whether an ad consent dialog appears (see 6.). The only thing passed on to Google is whether an ad request is to be treated as "under the age of consent" or "directed at children". The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest is to protect minors and to request ads only in the way permitted for their age (Art. 8 GDPR, § 25 TDDDG). The app cannot be used without this information.

6. Ad videos (Google AdMob) and consent

At the end of each region the app shows a short ad video that finances the free game; you start it with "Watch video". The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; for users outside the EEA and Switzerland Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA ("Google").

Preloading: So that a video starts right away, the app preloads one in the background after the start and after every video watched. The data listed below is already sent to Google then – even if you don't watch a video. If you bought the videos away, the app no longer loads any ads.

Data processed: your device's advertising ID (only with your consent and unless you deleted it), IP address, device and network information (e.g. model, operating system, language), app information, interactions with ads, approximate location derived from the IP address.

Purposes: serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud and abuse and – only with your consent – limiting how often ads are shown with the help of identifiers, and personalised advertising.

Consent: In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, after you have accepted the legal texts, the app asks you from the age of 16 for your consent via Google's consent platform (User Messaging Platform under the IAB standard "Transparency & Consent Framework"). With consent, Google may read and store identifiers on your device (e.g. the advertising ID) and – depending on your choice – serve personalised or non-personalised ads; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG.

Without consent the app only requests "limited ads": Google then uses no cookies, no advertising ID and no other identifiers or local storage on your device, and the ads are not personalised. Only the data needed for delivery is processed (e.g. IP address, device and app information) to serve and measure the ad and to prevent fraud; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (financing the free game, protection against fraud).

Withdrawal: You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future under Legal and data → Ad consent (the button appears where consent is asked for). The lawfulness of the processing carried out until then remains unaffected.

Minors: If you are younger than 16, no consent is asked for: the app then requests, worldwide, only limited ads without identifiers on your device and marks the requests as "under the age of consent"; there is no personalised advertising. Under 13 the requests are additionally marked as "directed at children" (COPPA) and only ads suitable for all ages are requested.

Google's role: Google processes this data as an independent controller (Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms). More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Third country: Google may transfer data to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the EU Commission, Art. 45 GDPR); in addition, Google uses standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

Advertising ID: You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Android's settings (Privacy → Ads).

7. Purchase through Google Play

The in-app purchase "Remove all videos from the game" is processed by Google Play; in the EU, the EEA and the UK Google Commerce Limited, Ireland, acts as the "merchant of record" (see Terms of Use § 2). We do not receive payment data. From Google Play the app only learns whether the purchase exists and what its status is; from this it stores locally that the videos are removed. At every start the app asks Google Play for existing purchases, so the purchase applies again after a reinstall. In the Play Console we see the order number, the product, the price, the country and the payment status of each purchase, but not your payment details. Purpose: performing the purchase contract, restoring the purchase and handling withdrawals and refunds; legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, for tax retention obligations Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. Google's privacy notice applies to the payment (https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice).

8. Download, statistics and crash reports (Google Play)

The app is distributed through Google Play. For download, installation and updates Google processes the data needed for this (e.g. your Google account and device data) under its own responsibility; Google's privacy policy applies. In the Play Console Google provides us with aggregated statistics (e.g. number of installs by country, device type and app version). If you have allowed Android to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, we also receive crash and performance reports (e.g. device model, Android version, app version, error log) from which we cannot identify you. We use them only to fix errors and improve the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Contact by e-mail and withdrawal in the app

If you write to us (e.g. support, privacy request, withdrawal, defect report), we process your e-mail address, your name (if given) and the content of your message in order to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns a purchase or the use of the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering requests). We delete the messages once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations (in particular under commercial and tax law) or the defence of claims require longer storage (Art. 6(1)(c) or (f) GDPR).

If you use the "Withdraw from contract" function in the app, we process your name, your e-mail address, the details of the contract (Google Play order number, purchase date), the language and version of the app and the date and time of receipt. The app transmits these details encrypted to a script service by Google (Google Apps Script) in our Google account. It sends us the withdrawal by e-mail and sends you the confirmation of receipt required by law without delay (§ 356a BGB); it does not store the details, which afterwards are only in our and your mailbox. If the service cannot be reached, the app offers to send the withdrawal to us with your e-mail app. The provider of the service is Google (see 6.); data may be transferred to the USA (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Art. 45 GDPR). The purpose is to handle your withdrawal and to prove that it was received in time; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We keep the withdrawal as long as claims under the contract can be asserted (usually three years from the end of the year of the withdrawal) and statutory retention obligations exist.

10. No online features, no automated decisions

All enemies are computer-controlled and calculated on your device; there are no leaderboards and no exchange with other players. We make no automated decisions within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR and create no profiles. Personalised advertising by Google is based on your consent (see 6.).

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Where processing is based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation – informally by e-mail to popcornbucket@freenet.de.

As we do not store any data about you ourselves, you can exercise most rights directly in the app (delete game data, change ad consent). For advertising and payment data please also contact Google (https://myaccount.google.com). Requests to us: popcornbucket@freenet.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the member state of your residence. The authority responsible for us is: Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg, Heilbronner Straße 35, 70191 Stuttgart, www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de.

12. Children and young people

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. Younger players are recognised through the age question and receive only limited advertising suitable for children, without identifiers on the device. Parents can control in-app purchases through Google Play's parental controls (Family Link, purchase approval).
