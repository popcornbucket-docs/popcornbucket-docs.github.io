Delete your data

Last updated: 18.11.2026

Speed Ninja by David Rene Meinke has no user account and no server of its own. You delete your progress yourself in the app. Data held by us (only if you wrote to us or withdrew from a purchase) we delete at your request by e-mail to popcornbucket@freenet.de.

1. Delete your progress on your device

  • In the app: start screen → Legal and data → Delete game data. The app then closes and starts like a fresh install the next time you open it.
  • Or clear the app data of Speed Ninja in Android's settings or uninstall the app.

Deleted immediately and completely: the progress of all four ninjas, coins, scrolls, weapons, talismans, healing potions, settings, the accepted version of the legal texts, your age group and your ad consent. Nothing of it is kept. The purchase "Remove all videos from the game" stays tied to your Google account and is restored at the next start with a connection to Google Play (or under Legal and data → Restore purchases). If you have switched on Android backup, a copy of your progress may be in your Google account; you manage and delete it in the backup settings of Android or your Google account.

2. Request deletion of data held by us

We only hold data if you wrote to us or used "Withdraw from contract" in the app: your name, your e-mail address, your message and details of the purchase (Google Play order number, purchase date).

  • Send an e-mail to popcornbucket@freenet.de with the subject "Speed Ninja – Delete data".
  • Give the e-mail address you wrote to us from and – if available – the order number.

We delete the data without delay, at the latest within one month, and confirm the deletion to you. We only have to keep a withdrawal as long as claims under the contract can be asserted (usually three years from the end of the year of the withdrawal) and records of purchases and refunds as long as commercial and tax law require (six to ten years depending on the record). Until then we use them for nothing else; afterwards we delete them.

3. Data held by Google

Google processes advertising data (Google AdMob), purchase and payment data (Google Play) and the Android backup under its own responsibility; we cannot delete them.

  • Reset or delete the advertising ID: Android settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Change or withdraw ad consent: in the app under Legal and data → Ad consent (the button appears where consent is asked for).
  • View and delete data held by Google: https://myaccount.google.com

Aggregated statistics and crash reports from Google Play cannot be linked to a person. More about all data is in the Privacy Policy.
