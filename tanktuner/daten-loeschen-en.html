Delete your data

Effective: 14.10.2026

TankTuner by David Rene Meinke has no user account and no server of its own. You delete your game data yourself in the app. Data we hold (only if you wrote to us or withdrew from a purchase) we delete on request by e-mail to popcornbucket@freenet.de.

1. Delete your game data on your device

  • In the app: Settings → Legal and data → Delete game data.
  • Or clear TankTuner's app data in the Android settings or uninstall the app.

This deletes immediately and completely: player name, progress, in-game currency, tanks, drivers, parts, weapons, settings, the version of the accepted legal texts, your age group and your ad consent. Nothing of it is kept. The "All drivers" purchase stays tied to your Google account and can be unlocked again under Settings → Legal and data → Restore purchases. If you have switched on the Android backup, a copy of your game data may be stored in your Google account; you manage and delete it in the backup settings of Android or your Google account.

2. Request deletion of the data we hold

We only hold data if you wrote to us or used "Withdraw from contract" in the app: your name, your e-mail address, your message and details of the purchase (Google Play order number, purchase date).

  • Send an e-mail to popcornbucket@freenet.de with the subject "TankTuner – delete my data".
  • State the e-mail address you wrote to us from and, if you have one, the order number.

We delete the data without undue delay, at the latest within one month, and confirm the deletion to you. We only have to keep a withdrawal as long as claims under the contract can be asserted (usually three years from the end of the year of the withdrawal) and records of purchases and refunds as long as commercial and tax law requires (six to ten years, depending on the record). Until then we use them for nothing else; afterwards we delete them.

3. Data held by Google

Google processes advertising data (Google AdMob), purchase and payment data (Google Play) and the Android backup as an independent controller; we cannot delete them.

  • Reset or delete the advertising ID: Android settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Change or withdraw your ad consent: in the app under Settings → Legal and data → Ad consent (the button appears wherever consent is requested).
  • View and delete your data at Google: https://myaccount.google.com

Aggregated statistics and crash reports from Google Play cannot be linked to a person. More about all data is in the privacy policy.
