Privacy Policy

Effective: 14.10.2026 · Version 3

In short: TankTuner needs no account. Your progress stays on your device. We ourselves receive only a little data: details of purchases (e.g. order number, price, country) and aggregated statistics through Google Play – and your details if you write to us or withdraw from a purchase. Data goes to Google: for the rewarded videos (personalised only with your consent), for purchases through Google Play and – if you switched it on – for Android backup.

2. Overview

  • No user account, no registration, no server of our own.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own. From Google Play we only receive aggregated statistics and crash reports that cannot be linked to you (see 8.).
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • Permissions: internet and network state (for rewarded videos and Google Play), advertising ID and Android's advertising interfaces (for Google AdMob), Google Play billing (for purchases) and technical permissions for background tasks of these Google libraries. None of them gives access to your personal content; you do not have to grant any of them individually.
  • Data is only shared with Google (ads via AdMob, purchases and statistics via Google Play, Android backup); if you visit our website, also with GitHub (see 16.).

3. Game progress on your device

The app stores locally, in the protected app storage of your device: your player name, progress (level, experience, rank points, challenges beaten and due, in-game currency, tanks, drivers – including those unlocked by the "All drivers" purchase –, parts, weapons, skills, battle team, recruit and tank tokens, free openings, missions, milestones, statistics, progress in the introduction and events, daily counters for rewarded videos and tuning), settings (language, home screen, trajectory preview), the version of the legal texts you accepted and your age group. Google's consent platform also stores your ad consent choice there (see 6.).

This data never leaves your device towards the provider. Its only purpose is to provide the game to you. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract for using the app); storing it on your device is strictly necessary for the service you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG / Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive).

Retention: until you delete the data in the app (Settings → Legal and data → Delete game data; this also deletes the stored ad consent), clear the app data in the Android settings or uninstall the app.

4. Android backup

If Android backup ("Backup by Google One") is switched on, Android may back up your progress encrypted to your Google account and restore it on a new device; when you switch devices, Android may also transfer it directly to the new device. You control this backup yourself in the Android settings; it is provided by Google and Google's privacy policy applies (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The legal basis for allowing the backup is our legitimate interest in sparing you the loss of your progress when you change devices (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. First start (age check)

At the first start the app asks for your year of birth. Only the age group is derived from it (under 13, 13–15, 16 and over) and stored locally – the year of birth itself is neither stored nor transmitted. The age group controls which ads may be requested and whether an ad consent dialog appears (see 6.). The only thing passed to Google is whether an ad request is to be treated as "under the age of consent" or "child-directed". Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR together with Art. 8 GDPR and the rules on the protection of minors, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (protection of minors). The app cannot be used without this information.

6. Rewarded videos (Google AdMob) and consent

The app offers optional rewarded videos (e.g. in-game currency or a tank for watching a video). A video is only shown when you start it yourself. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; for users outside the EEA and Switzerland Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA ("Google").

  • Preloading: So that a video starts right away, the app loads one in the background after the start and after every video watched. The data listed below is already sent to Google at that point – even if you do not watch a video.
  • Data processed: your device's advertising ID (unless you deleted it), IP address, device and network information (e.g. model, operating system, language), app information, interactions with ads, approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Purposes: serving and measuring ads, limiting how often ads are shown, preventing fraud and abuse and – only with your consent – personalised advertising.
  • Consent: In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent after the start through Google's consent platform (User Messaging Platform based on the IAB "Transparency & Consent Framework"). The legal basis is then Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. Without consent, only non-personalised or "limited" ads are served, to the extent Google allows this without consent (legal basis then Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: financing the free game, fraud prevention).
  • Withdrawal: You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future under Settings → Legal and data → Ad consent (the button appears wherever consent is requested). This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.
  • Minors: If you are under 16, no consent is requested and requests are tagged as "under the age of consent"; there is no personalised advertising. Under 13, requests are additionally tagged as "child-directed" (COPPA) and only ads suitable for all ages are requested.
  • Google's role: Google processes this data as an independent controller (Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms). More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Third countries: Google may transfer data to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR); in addition Google uses standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in the Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

7. Purchases through Google Play

The in-app purchase ("All drivers") is processed by Google Play; in the EU, the EEA and the UK the seller is Google Commerce Limited, Ireland (see Terms of Use § 2). We never receive payment data. The app only learns from Google Play whether a purchase exists, which product was bought and the status of the purchase (including a signed purchase receipt that the app verifies on your device). The unlock is then stored locally. In the Play Console we see the order number, product, price, country and payment status of each purchase, but not your payment details. If you write to us about a purchase, we can find the order there by its order number or your e-mail address. Purpose: performing the purchase contract, restoring purchases and handling withdrawals and refunds; legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, for statutory tax retention duties Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. Google's privacy notice applies to the payment (https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice).

8. Download, statistics and crash reports (Google Play)

The app is distributed through Google Play. For download, installation and updates, Google processes the data required for this (e.g. your Google account and device data) as an independent controller; Google's privacy policy applies. Google provides us with aggregated statistics in the Play Console (e.g. number of installations by country, device type and app version). If you allowed Android to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, we also receive crash and performance reports (e.g. device model, Android version, app version, error log) that do not allow us to identify you. We only use them to fix bugs and improve the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You control the sharing of diagnostics data in the Android settings.

9. Contact by e-mail and withdrawal in the app

If you write to us (e.g. support, privacy request, withdrawal, defect notice), we process your e-mail address, your name (if given) and the content of your message to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns a purchase or the use of the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering enquiries). We delete the messages once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention duties (in particular under commercial and tax law) or the defence of legal claims require longer storage (Art. 6(1)(c) or (f) GDPR).

If you use the "Withdraw from contract" function in the app, we process your name, your e-mail address, the contract details (Google Play order number, purchase date), the language and version of the app and the date and time of receipt. The app transmits these details encrypted to a script service of Google (Google Apps Script) in our Google account. It sends us the withdrawal by e-mail and sends you the confirmation of receipt required by law without delay (§ 356a BGB); it does not store the details – afterwards they are only in our and your mailbox. The provider of the service is Google (see 6.); data may be transferred to the USA in the process (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Art. 45 GDPR). The purpose is to process your withdrawal and to prove that it was received in time; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (statutory duty to provide the withdrawal function and the confirmation of receipt) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We keep the withdrawal as long as claims from the contract can be asserted (usually three years from the end of the year of the withdrawal) and statutory retention duties apply.

10. Ranking and opponents

The ranking and all opponents are currently calculated entirely on your device; opponents are computer-controlled. Your player name and results are not transmitted and are not visible to others. Should online features (e.g. rankings with real players) be added in the future, we will inform you in the app beforehand and update this policy; no data will be transmitted before that.

11. No automated decisions by us

We do not make automated decisions within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR and do not create profiles. Personalised advertising by Google is based on your consent (see 6.).

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Where processing is based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation – informally by e-mail to popcornbucket@freenet.de. You can put this into effect yourself by switching off Android backup or the sharing of diagnostics data and, for ads, refusing consent and deleting the advertising ID; you play entirely without ad requests to Google when your device is offline.

As we do not store any data about you ourselves, you can exercise most rights directly in the app (delete game data, change ad consent). For advertising and payment data, please also contact Google (https://myaccount.google.com). Requests to us: popcornbucket@freenet.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the member state of your residence. The authority responsible for us is: Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg, Heilbronner Straße 35, 70191 Stuttgart, www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de.

13. Children and teenagers

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. Younger players are identified by the age check and only receive non-personalised, child-appropriate ads. Parents can control in-app purchases through Google Play's parental controls (Family Link, purchase approval).

14. Data security

Your progress is kept in protected app storage that other apps cannot access. Connections of the Google services are encrypted (TLS). Purchases are only unlocked when the Google Play receipt carries a valid signature.
